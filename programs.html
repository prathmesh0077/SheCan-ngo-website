What We Do

Programs Designed for Impact

Five interconnected program areas, each addressing a critical dimension of women's empowerment.

📚
Education Programs

From basic literacy to higher education scholarships, we break the biggest barrier to women's advancement. Our education programs serve girls and women from age 8 to 55, meeting them where they are and taking them where they want to go.

Literacy Scholarships Mentorship
Learn More →
💼
Women Entrepreneurship

Our flagship entrepreneurship program provides business education, seed funding support, mentorship from established entrepreneurs, and access to market networks. 180+ women-owned businesses launched to date.

Business Skills Seed Grants Networks
Learn More →
🛠️
Skill Development

Practical, job-ready skills training in areas ranging from digital literacy and coding to tailoring, carpentry, hospitality, and healthcare. We partner with industry to ensure our training leads to real employment opportunities.

Digital Skills Vocational Certification
Learn More →
👑
Leadership Workshops

Our intensive leadership workshops build public speaking, negotiation, community organizing, and governance skills. Graduates have gone on to lead panchayats, run NGOs, and serve in state-level policy roles.

Public Speaking Governance Advocacy
Learn More →
🏘️
Community Outreach

We meet women where they live — running mobile workshops, community health camps, legal awareness sessions, and self-help group formation drives in rural and urban underserved communities.

Rural Outreach Health Legal Aid
Learn More →
💻
Digital Empowerment

In partnership with tech companies, we provide devices, internet access, and digital skills training to women in connectivity-deprived areas — opening doors to e-commerce, remote work, and digital banking.

Internet Access Coding E-Commerce

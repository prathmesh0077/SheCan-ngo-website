About She Can Foundation

Our Story, Our Mission, Our People

Founded in 2018, She Can Foundation has grown from a small community initiative into a movement that has touched thousands of lives across India.

Home / About Us
Who We Are

A Movement Rooted in Belief

She Can Foundation began with a single workshop in Nashik, Maharashtra — 12 women, one room, and the shared conviction that things could be different. Today, we operate across 14 states, running over 25 active programs that span education, economic empowerment, health literacy, and leadership development.

We are a registered non-profit organization committed to creating inclusive, sustainable change. Our work is guided by the women we serve — their voices shape our programs, our priorities, and our path forward.

We believe that when you invest in a woman, you invest in an entire community. That's not just our tagline — it's a truth we've watched unfold thousands of times.

Purpose & Direction

Vision & Mission

Two pillars that anchor everything we do.

🔭

Our Vision

A world where every woman — regardless of her background, location, or circumstance — has equal access to education, economic opportunity, and the freedom to lead the life she chooses.

We envision societies where gender is never a barrier, and where women's contributions are valued, celebrated, and built upon.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower women through education, skill development, and community building — creating pathways to economic independence, confident leadership, and lasting social change.

We do this by partnering with communities, listening to women's needs, and delivering programs that create real, measurable impact.

Our Journey

Milestones That Shaped Us

🌱
2018

The Seed Is Planted

Founded in Nashik with our first workshop serving 12 women. The response was overwhelming — a waiting list of 80 formed within a week.

📋
2019

Official Registration

Became a registered NGO and launched our first three formal programs: Basic Literacy, Vocational Skills, and Women's Health Awareness.

🏙️
2020

Expansion During Crisis

Despite the pandemic, we pivoted to digital delivery and reached 500+ women with online skill-building workshops across Maharashtra.

🤝
2021–22

National Partnerships

Formed partnerships with 6 state governments and 12 corporate CSR programs, scaling our reach to 8 new states.

🏆
2023

National NGO Excellence Award

Recognized as one of India's top 10 women's empowerment NGOs by the National Voluntary Organizations Council.

🌟
2024–26

5000+ Lives Transformed

Surpassed our 5,000 beneficiary milestone and launched our ambitious "She Leads 2030" campaign targeting 25,000 women across 25 states.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I support She Can Foundation without volunteering? +

You can donate, share our social media posts, connect us with potential partners, or advocate for women's empowerment in your community. Every action, big or small, makes a difference.

Are your programs available outside Maharashtra? +

Yes! We currently operate in 14 states across India and are actively expanding. Contact us to learn about programs in your region or to help us bring our work to new areas.

Can companies partner with She Can Foundation for CSR? +

Absolutely. We have robust CSR partnerships with over 20 companies. We offer co-branded programs, volunteer engagement, impact reporting, and public recognition. Email us at partnerships@shecanfoundation.org.

How quickly do you respond to messages? +

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24–48 business hours. For urgent matters, please call our office directly during business hours.
